Biography

I am an undergraduate student at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, pursuing a B.Eng. in Biomedical Science and Engineering with a minor in Artificial Intelligence. My current research focuses on EEG foundation models, representation probing, multimodal physiological signal modeling, and robust biomedical AI.

I am particularly interested in understanding what physiological information EEG foundation models actually encode, how peripheral signals such as EOG, EMG, and ECG interact with learned EEG representations, and how these insights can improve robustness and scaling.

Research Experience

NoisyBench: Probing Noise Robustness in EEG Foundation Models

2026 – Present

Researcher · Prof. Benyou Wang · CUHK-Shenzhen

Investigating whether EEG foundation models learn EEG-specific neurophysiological representations or encode peripheral physiological noise. I proposed a final-layer representation-probing framework for EEG-intrinsic and peripheral physiological features, together with a ratio-based metric for representation purity and noise robustness.

EEG-Based Epileptic Seizure Classification

Sep 2025 – May 2026

Undergraduate Researcher · Prof. Shixiong Chen · CUHK-Shenzhen

Led the project end-to-end and developed a multi-resolution, phase-aware spatio-temporal network that jointly models spectral, phase, spatial, and temporal information. Experiments used CHB-MIT and Siena with cross-validation, leave-one-subject-out evaluation, ablations, and t-SNE analysis.

EEG Emotion Recognition Across Healthy and Depressed Populations

Mar 2026 – Jul 2026

Research Project · 11th National College Student Biomedical Engineering Innovation Design Competition

Designed a multi-expert EEG framework using DE/PSD features, task-specific and shared channel-mixing experts, and reciprocal cross-attention to leverage auxiliary diagnosis information. The system achieved 70% accuracy in a leave-six-subject evaluation and received the National Third Prize.

Emotion-Neuromodulation Brain-Computer Interface

Jun 2026 – Aug 2026

Research Assistant · Prof. Raymond K. Y. Tong · The Chinese University of Hong Kong

Co-designed a fear-response neuromodulation paradigm, implemented and validated the E-Prime task, integrated stimulation and physiological recording equipment, and completed pilot EEG and heart-rate acquisition.

Detection of Salivary hCG Using Smartphone-Assisted Lateral Flow Assay

May 2024 – Feb 2025

Undergraduate Researcher · Prof. Guozhen Liu · CUHK-Shenzhen
